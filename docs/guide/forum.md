# Forum

The **Forum** is shared by all PSMS offices: a place to ask questions and share tips with other users.

- Browse by category on the left; the categories are managed by the PSMS administrator.
- **New post** starts a topic. Write in plain text; line breaks are kept.
- Reply at the bottom of a post, and **Like** posts you find helpful.
- You can edit or delete your own posts, and delete your own replies.

::: warning
Other offices can read the forum. Don't post contract amounts, personal information or anything confidential.
:::
