# Forum

The **Forum** is a place to ask questions and share tips with PSMS users from **all offices**. Everyone with a PSMS
account can read and post, whatever their role.

::: warning The forum is shared
Other offices can read everything here, and posts show your **username**. Don't post contract amounts, names of
contractors, personal information or anything confidential.
:::

## Browsing

Open **Forum** in the sidebar. Posts are listed newest first, 30 per page, each with its category, author, date, number
of replies and likes. Use the **Categories** list on the left to show one category, or **All**:

- **Announcements:** news from the PSMS team, such as new features and maintenance.
- **Usage Queries:** "how do I...?" questions about using PSMS.
- **Feature Requests:** ideas for improving PSMS.
- **General Discussions:** anything else.

The categories are managed by the PSMS administrator.

![A forum post with a reply](/screens/forum-post.jpg)

## Posting

1. Click **New post** (if you're viewing a category, it's chosen for you).
2. Pick the **category**, give the post a clear **title** and write your message. It's plain text: line breaks are
   kept, but there's no formatting or attachments.
3. Click **Post**.

Good posts say what you were trying to do, what you expected and what happened, for example *"Recording accomplishment
during a suspension"* rather than *"Help"*.

## Replying and liking

- Open a post to read the replies, oldest first, and type yours in the box at the bottom, then click **Post reply**.
- **Like** a post to show it was helpful; click again (**Unlike**) to take it back. The number shows how many people
  liked it.

## Editing and deleting

- You can **edit** or **delete** your own posts with the links under the post. Deleting a post also deletes its replies
  and can't be undone.
- You can **delete** your own replies; replies can't be edited, so delete and post again to correct one.
- The PSMS administrator can edit or remove any post or reply.

::: tip Need help from the PSMS team?
Post in **Usage Queries**. For anything about your office's own data or subscription, contact the PSMS administrator
directly instead of posting it in the forum.
:::
