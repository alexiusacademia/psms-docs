# Signing in and finding your way

## Signing in

Go to **[app.psms.ph](https://app.psms.ph)** and sign in with the username and password your office's
Account Holder gave you.

![The sign-in page](/screens/sign-in.jpg)

- **Forgot your password?** Ask your office's Account Holder. They can set a new one for you (see [Users](/guide/team#editing-a-user)).
- **Change your password** any time from the menu under your name (top right) → **Change password**.
- **New office?** Choose **Start a 30-day free trial** on the sign-in page to register your office. You'll need the
  office's address, phone, email and a contact person. You become its [Account Holder](/guide/roles) and can add your team.

## The layout

- **Sidebar (left):** your office name and renewal date, then the main pages (**Dashboard, Contracts, Projects,
  Reports, Forum**), the **Setup** lists, and **Office** pages if you're an Account Holder. On a phone, open it with
  the menu button (☰) at the top left. **Help & user guide** at the bottom opens this guide.
- **Top bar:** the search box, the **?** button and your name. **?** opens the page of this guide for the screen you're
  on. Click your name for **My activity**, **Change password**, **Help & user guide** and **Log out**.

## Search (Ctrl K / ⌘ K)

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows) or **⌘ K** (Mac), or click the search box, and start typing a contract number, project,
contractor, system or municipality. Results appear as you type; click one to open it. Press **Esc** to close.

![Searching for "malinaw"](/screens/search.jpg)

## Adding and editing records

Buttons like **Record accomplishment**, **Add billing** or the pencil icon open a small **dialog** over the page.
Fill it in and click **Save**; the page refreshes and a confirmation appears in the bottom-right corner.
If something is wrong (for example a date in the future), the dialog stays open and shows what to fix.

![A dialog for recording accomplishment](/screens/dialog.jpg)

Deleting always asks you to confirm first. Contracts, projects, systems, fund sources, expenditures and variation
orders are **not permanently erased**: they disappear from lists and reports but stay in the database, so the PSMS administrator can recover
them if needed. Items that other records still use (for example a contractor with contracts) can't be deleted.

## Dates and "as of"

Most pages and every report have an **As of** date. PSMS shows everything as it stood on that date: accomplishment,
suspensions, variation orders, time extensions and billings recorded after it are left out. It defaults to today.
