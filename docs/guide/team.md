# Users and team activity

These pages are under **Office** in the sidebar and are for **Account Holders**.

## Users

**Office → Users** lists everyone in your office with their position, roles, whether they're active, and their last
sign-in. Click a username to see their [activity](#a-person-s-activity).

![The users list](/screens/users-list.jpg)

### Adding a user

Click **Add user** and enter a username, name, email, position, one or more [roles](/guide/roles) and a password.
Give the user their username and password; they can change the password after signing in. Add their **email** too:
it's how they can reset a forgotten password themselves.

### Editing a user

Click the pencil icon to change details or roles, or to **set a new password** (for example when someone forgets
theirs). Leave the password fields blank to keep the current one.

![Editing a user's roles](/screens/user-edit-roles.jpg)

### Removing a user

Untick **Active** instead of deleting. The user can no longer sign in, but everything they recorded keeps their name.
You can't deactivate yourself.

## Team activity

**Office → Team activity** shows a calendar for each person: one square per day, darker for busier days, over the last
12 months or a chosen year. All rows use the same shading, so you can compare people. People with no activity in the
period are listed at the bottom.

![Team activity](/screens/team-activity.jpg)

"Activity" counts entries, edits and deletions. Sign-ins are counted separately and don't make a day look busy.

## A person's activity

Click a name (in Team activity or Users) to see their calendar with active days, busiest day, longest and current
streak, sign-ins, and what kinds of changes they made. **Click a day** to see exactly what they did that day.

![A person's activity](/screens/user-profile.jpg)

Everyone can see **their own** activity from the menu under their name → **My activity**. Only Account Holders can see
other people's.

## Activity log

**Office → Activity log** is the complete record of who changed what and when, including sign-ins, with the old and new
values for edits. Filter by person or type of action, or search.
