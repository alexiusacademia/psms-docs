# Frequently asked questions

## Where do I sign in?

At **[app.psms.ph](https://app.psms.ph)**. The old address, psms.ph, forwards there.

## I forgot my password.

Ask your office's Account Holder to set a new one (**Office → Users** → pencil icon), then change it yourself from
the menu under your name → **Change password**.

## Why can't I edit an accomplishment entry I made last week?

Accomplishment entries, billings, variation orders and physical-feature quantities can be changed only for **one day**
after they're entered; after that only an Account Holder can. See [the one-day rule](/guide/roles#the-one-day-rule).

## Why won't PSMS accept my accomplishment?

Common reasons: the date is in the future; the value is lower than an earlier entry or higher than a later one
(accomplishment is cumulative); the contract was suspended on that date; or there's already an entry for that date
(edit it instead).

## Can a contract be deleted?

Yes. Account Holders, Project Inspectors and Engineering Personnel can delete a contract from its page (the menu next
to **Timeline**). It disappears from lists and reports but stays in the database, so the PSMS administrator can recover
it if needed.

## Is there a separate schedule node for time extensions?

No. Record the extension under the contract's **Time extensions** tab. It moves the *expiry due to time extensions* and
adds to the contract time. If the planned schedule itself changes, edit the **Schedule** tab.

## Why is the revised plan flat on the S-curve?

The contract is (or was) suspended. The revised plan pauses while work is suspended and continues when it resumes.

## Why is a contract missing from a report?

Contract reports leave out contracts **without a contractor** and work **by administration** (a contractor named
"Administration"). Also check the implementation year: it comes from the contract's **project**.

## Why can't I see another office's data?

Each office's data is private to it. Only contractors and the forum are shared between offices.

## Our office shows a renewal notice.

The subscription's renewal date has passed. Nothing has been deleted; contact the PSMS administrator to renew and
access returns immediately. See [Subscription](/guide/office#subscription).
